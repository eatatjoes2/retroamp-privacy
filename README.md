# RetroAmp Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

RetroAmp is a music and audiobook player for iPhone and Apple TV that plays media from a Plex Media Server you have access to. It is free, has no ads, and has no accounts of its own.

## What RetroAmp collects

Nothing. The developer of RetroAmp does not collect, receive, store, or sell any information about you or how you use the app. RetroAmp contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and it has no servers of its own.

## What stays on your device

- **Your Plex sign-in.** When you link the app to your Plex account, Plex gives the app an access token. It is stored in the device's Keychain and is removed when you sign out.
- **Your settings.** Preferences such as the equalizer, volume, chosen server, and the current play queue are stored on the device so the app can pick up where you left off.

## Who the app talks to

RetroAmp connects only to:

- **Plex (plex.tv)**, to sign you in and to find the Plex servers your account can reach.
- **The Plex Media Server you choose**, to browse the library and play media. Like any Plex player, the app reports playback progress to that server so your play history and resume positions stay in sync. On iPhone, the app asks for local network access so it can reach a server on your home network directly.

Information you share with Plex or with a server's owner is handled under their terms, not this policy. Plex's privacy policy is at https://www.plex.tv/about/privacy-legal/.

## TestFlight

If you install a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer. That is governed by Apple's TestFlight terms.

## Children

RetroAmp is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
